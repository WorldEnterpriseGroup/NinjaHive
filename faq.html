Everything You Need to Know

Common questions about accessing Ninja Hive, using our tools, and joining the ninja community

Ninja Hive is exclusively available to Developer Dojo incubator participants. Access is highly selective and granted only to those accepted into a Developer Dojo incubator program. This ensures our community maintains a high standard of collaboration and innovation.

No, graduation from Developer Dojo does not automatically grant Ninja Hive access. You must be accepted into the Developer Dojo incubator program. The incubator is a selective continuation program focused on building real-world projects with mentorship and community support.

Focus Pass is your membership to the entire Focus Hive ecosystem, which includes Ninja Hive. It provides access to cloud development tools, virtual environments, and collaboration platforms. Different Focus Pass tiers (Starter, Pro, Elite) offer varying levels of resources and capabilities to match your project needs.

Contact your Developer Dojo instructor to express your interest in the incubator program. The incubator is highly selective and looks for developers who are committed to building meaningful projects and contributing to the community. Demonstrated skill, dedication, and collaborative spirit are key factors in acceptance.

Vibe jams are collaborative coding sessions where ninjas work together on projects, share knowledge, and build community. They're informal, creative spaces for pair programming, brainstorming, and mutual support. Participating actively in vibe jams is one pathway to earning access to Ninja Hive and building your reputation within the community.

Any device with a modern web browser can access Ninja Hive. This includes laptops, tablets, Chromebooks, and even smartphones. Our cloud-based platform means you're not limited by your device's specifications - all the heavy computing happens on our infrastructure.

Use your Focus Pass credentials to log in to Ninja Hive. Once you're accepted into a Developer Dojo incubator and have your Focus Pass membership set up, you'll receive access instructions. Simply visit the platform and sign in with your Focus Pass account to get started.

For most coding tasks, Code Dojo is your go-to tool - it's a powerful browser-based VS Code IDE perfect for development. Use Virtual Dojo when you need a full operating system environment, specialized software installations, or tools that require desktop-level access. Many ninjas use both depending on their current project needs.

Code Dojo is a browser-based VS Code IDE powered by code-server. It provides a full-featured development environment accessible from anywhere with an internet connection. You get all the power of VS Code - extensions, terminal access, git integration, and debugging - without any local installation required.

Virtual Dojo provides full desktop environments accessible through your browser via Apache Guacamole. This gives you complete virtual machines where you can install any software, configure complex environments, and work with applications that require a full OS. It's perfect for advanced development scenarios and specialized tooling.

Ninja Hive includes a complete suite of tools for building production-ready applications: databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB), deployment platforms, testing frameworks, monitoring and analytics tools, API development tools, and version control systems. Everything you need to take your project from concept to deployment.

Absolutely! Collaboration is at the heart of Ninja Hive. You can participate in vibe jams, join community channels on Facebook and WhatsApp, engage in pair programming sessions, and get support from fellow ninjas and Master Ninjas. Our platform is designed to foster community and collaborative learning.

The Focus Pass tier you need depends on your resource requirements. Starter tier works for basic projects, Pro tier offers more compute power and storage for larger applications, and Elite tier provides maximum resources for complex, production-scale projects. Your instructor can help you determine which tier fits your needs.

Yes, you can upgrade your Focus Pass tier at any time by visiting focuspass.com. This is useful when your project grows and you need more resources, or when you want to access additional features. Upgrades typically take effect immediately.

Technical support is available through multiple channels: our community channels on Facebook and WhatsApp where fellow ninjas and Master Ninjas can help, direct support from your Developer Dojo instructor, and our support email at support@ninjahive.org. The community is very responsive and supportive.

For membership management questions including pausing your account, contact Focus Pass directly through focuspass.com. They handle all billing and membership administration. Note that pausing may affect your access to ongoing projects and community activities.

Connect with the ninja community through our Facebook group, WhatsApp channels, and vibe jam sessions. These platforms are where ninjas share knowledge, collaborate on projects, troubleshoot together, and build lasting professional relationships. Active participation in the community is key to getting the most out of Ninja Hive.

Share your projects in the community channels, present them during vibe jams, and contribute to the collective knowledge base. Showcasing your work helps you get feedback, inspire others, build your portfolio, and establish yourself as an active community member. Many ninjas have launched successful projects that started as community demos.

Master Ninjas are experienced community members who have demonstrated exceptional skill, contribution, and dedication. They mentor newer ninjas, lead vibe jams, contribute to platform improvements, and help maintain the collaborative culture. They're an invaluable resource for guidance and support.

Becoming a Master Ninja happens through active participation, helping other community members, building meaningful contributions to the platform or shared resources, and demonstrating leadership in vibe jams and community initiatives. It's a recognition earned over time through consistent, quality engagement with the Ninja Hive community.
