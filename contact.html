FAQs

Got Questions? We Have Answers!

Get answers to common questions about accessing the hive and community support.

Access to Ninja Hive requires a Focus Pass membership. If you're a Developer Dojo graduate or incubator participant, talk to your instructor about getting access. Visit focuspass.com to learn more.

Join our community on Facebook and WhatsApp to connect with fellow ninjas. You can also participate in vibe jams to collaborate, ask questions, and get support from Master Ninjas and other community members.

Developer Dojo is our training program that teaches the skills you need to become a ninja. Graduates can join the Ninja Hive incubator to turn their ideas into deployed applications. Visit developerdojo.org to learn more about training programs.

Vibe jams are collaborative sessions where ninjas come together to work on projects, share knowledge, and support each other. They're a great way to get help, learn new skills, and connect with the community. Email vibejams@ninjahive.org for more information.

For technical support with any Ninja Hive platform tools, email support@ninjahive.org with details about your issue. You can also ask for help in our community channels where fellow ninjas and Master Ninjas may be able to assist.

Absolutely! We respect ninja anonymity. You're welcome to use a pseudonym or ninja name in our community channels. Many ninjas prefer to keep their real identity private while collaborating on projects and sharing knowledge.

Ninjas have access to Code Dojo (cloud IDE), Virtual Dojo (virtual machines), ML Forge (machine learning platform), and collaborative workspaces. These tools help you build and deploy your applications from anywhere. Check our Services page for more details.

The incubator program is typically available to Developer Dojo graduates and current students. Contact your Developer Dojo instructor to learn about incubator opportunities and how to get involved with turning your ideas into real applications.

Focus Pass is your membership to access Ninja Hive's platform and tools. It provides secure authentication and access management for all our services. Visit focuspass.com to learn more about membership options.

Master Ninjas are experienced community members who provide guidance, technical support, and mentorship to other ninjas. They participate in vibe jams and community channels to help fellow ninjas overcome challenges and grow their skills.
